## Description (4000)

Crezo is the business side of being a creator, in one app built for India.

BRAND DEALS
Track every collaboration from lead to paid. See what you have earned and what
is still pending for each financial year, filter deals by stage, and never lose
track of a deliverable or deadline.

GST INVOICES
Turn a finished deal into an invoice in one tap. Add your GSTIN, PAN, UPI and
bank details once and every invoice is ready to send as a PDF, including over
WhatsApp. Not registered for GST? You can still invoice.

CONTENT CALENDAR
Plan reels, videos and stories on a month or week view, tag each post to the
deal it is for, and get a reminder before it is due. Subscribe to your plan
from Google or Apple Calendar.

MEDIA KIT
Get your own page at crezo.studio/yourname with the platforms, reach and rates
you choose to show. Update it in the app and the link stays the same. No more
exporting PDFs from Canva.

MEDIA VAULT
Sort your camera roll into folders for each brand deal. Your photos and videos
never leave your phone. Crezo only remembers which clip belongs where.

EARLY ACCESS
Every feature is free while Crezo is in early access. Early members keep Pro
free for three months after paid plans launch.

Sign in with Apple or Google. Your data belongs to you, and you can delete
your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Tracking: **No**. No data is used to track users across apps or websites. No
third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs are included.

Data collected, all **linked to the user**, all for **App Functionality** only:

| Apple category | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info: Name | Your name | Profile, invoices, media kit |
| Contact Info: Email Address | Sign-in email (Apple or Google) | Account |
| Contact Info: Physical Address | Your billing address | Printed on invoices |
| Financial Info: Other Financial Info | GSTIN, PAN, UPI ID, bank account and IFSC | Printed on invoices |
| User Content: Other User Content | Deals, content plans, invoices, media kit text and profile photo | The app's core records |
| Identifiers: User ID | Account ID | Account |

Not collected: photos or videos (the vault stores only a reference to each item
on the device), location, contacts, browsing history, health, purchases, usage
analytics, diagnostics.

## Notes for App Review

> Sign in with Apple or Google on the first screen; no demo account is needed.
> All features are free during early access and the app contains no purchases.
>
> Photo library access is requested only when the Vault tab is opened. The
> Vault lets creators group existing photos and videos into folders per brand
> deal; it stores a reference to each item, and no media is uploaded.
>
> Account deletion: Profile > Privacy & security > Delete my account. It removes
> the account and all of its data.
